Our story

A neighborhood table built around one oven

Cedar & Coal started with a simple idea: cook honest food over real fire and serve it in a room where everyone feels like a regular. Our oak-burning hearth runs from open to close, and our menu follows what local growers bring to the back door each week.

Come for a date night, a birthday, or a Tuesday. We will save you a seat by the fire.
